## Tools

### [Frosty HSL](https://tfp.vitrem.org/FrostyHSL) ([GitHub](https://github.com/thefrostypixel/FrostyHSL))
A hex and HSL color picker utilizing perceptually uniform saturation and lightness to keep colors consistent accross different hues.

### [URL-Encoder](https://tfp.vitrem.org/URL-Encoder) ([GitHub](https://github.com/thefrostypixel/URL-Encoder))
An online utility for converting text or files into Data-URLs. It features a custom MIME-type selection and automatically identifies the most efficient encoding scheme.

### [ViewVideo](https://tfp.vitrem.org/ViewVideo) ([GitHub](https://github.com/thefrostypixel/ViewVideo))
A web-based local video-file player with more practical and feature-rich UI than most browser-built-in and pre-installed viedo-players.<br>
It tracks, saves and restores progress for multiple files separately and automatically detects and groups sequential files such as “Video 1.mp4” and “Video 2.mp4”.

### [Font-Installer](https://tfp.vitrem.org/Font-Installer) ([GitHub](https://github.com/thefrostypixel/Font-Installer))
An in-browser tool to generate and load the necessary configuration profiles to install .otf and .ttf files system-wide on iOS and iPadOS.

### [RandomText](https://tfp.vitrem.org/RandomText) ([GitHub](https://github.com/thefrostypixel/RandomText))
A pseudo-text generator that uses English character frequency and statistical distribution to generate placeholder-text that looks like English without containing actual words that could distract viewers.

### [TOTPAuth](https://tfp.vitrem.org/TOTPAuth) ([GitHub](https://github.com/thefrostypixel/TOTPAuth))
A local-first TOTP (Time-based One-Time Password) manager. It handles authentication token generation and storage entirely within the browser’s local storage.

## Games

### [Impostor](https://tfp.vitrem.org/Impostor) ([GitHub](https://github.com/thefrostypixel/Impostor))
A browser-based social deduction game where most players receive a secret word but the “impostors” do not.<br>
Players must describe the word vaguely enough that the impostors don’t guess it but specifically enough to prove they aren’t the impostor.
